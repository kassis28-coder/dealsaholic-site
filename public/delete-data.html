Deals-aholic

Request deletion of your data

You may request deletion of data associated with your Deals-aholic community profile or deal-alert preferences.

How to request deletion

Email inquiresonthego@gmail.com with the subject line “Deals-aholic data deletion request”. Include the display name used in the app and, if applicable, the alert keyword or category you used.

What we delete

We will remove your community profile identifier, display name, comments and replies, reactions, reports, and deal-alert preferences or notification token associated with your request. We may retain limited records when needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Timing

We aim to process verified requests within 30 days. You can also stop future deal alerts at any time from the Alerts screen in the app.
